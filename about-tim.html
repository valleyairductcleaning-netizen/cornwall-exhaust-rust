Years of Hands-On Experience

My name is Tim. I started working on vehicles when I was 18, and over the years I’ve spent countless hours dealing with rusted bolts, seized parts, broken exhaust pipes and all the challenges that come with working on cars in Ontario.

Rust and exhaust problems have been two of the most constant battles. They can turn a simple repair into a difficult job, but that is also the kind of challenge I enjoy.

Why I Like Refurbishing Exhaust Parts

One of my favourite things is taking an exhaust part that looks worn out and seeing whether it can be refurbished. I enjoy cutting out damaged sections, fitting pipe and welding sound metal back together.

I believe in looking at what can be saved before telling someone they need to replace everything. Not every component is repairable, and safety comes first, but I like finding a sensible solution when one exists.

Why I Started Cornwall Exhaust & Rust

I wanted to focus on the work I know well: exhaust repairs, welding, rust problems and undercoating. My goal is to provide honest work, fair pricing and repairs I can stand behind.

Faith and Integrity

As a Christian, I believe how I treat people matters just as much as the work I do. I want to treat my neighbours fairly, be straightforward about repairs and do my work to the glory of God.
