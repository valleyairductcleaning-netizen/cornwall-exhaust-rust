Meet Tim

I'm Tim. Cornwall Exhaust & Rust is a small family business, and for now my workshop is the driveway. I work on exhausts, rust repairs and undercoating right here in Cornwall, Ontario.

The Life of a Driveway Mechanic

I've worked on vehicles since I was 18. Rusted bolts, broken exhaust pipes and seized parts have kept me busy for years. I enjoy fixing what can be repaired rather than automatically replacing everything.

Would I love a proper garage, a hoist and a warm place to work when winter comes? Absolutely. But a garage isn't in the cards right now, so I work with what I've got. Some days that means sunshine; other days it's rain, snow and a cold driveway.

A FAMILY BUSINESS

Small Business. Big Reason to Keep Going.

My wife Caitlin and our son Elijah are a big part of why I do this. We're not a big repair chain or a fancy garage. We're a family trying to make an honest living and do good work for our neighbours.

When you get in touch, you're dealing with a real person. We take on exhaust and muffler repairs, welding, rust work and undercoating, and we talk through what makes sense for your vehicle.

Why I Enjoy Exhaust and Rust Work

There's something satisfying about taking a worn-out exhaust part, cutting away the damaged metal and welding in a sound repair. I also enjoy tackling the rust problems that come with Ontario vehicles.

Not everything can or should be repaired. Safety matters, and I'll be straightforward about what I can do.

Maybe One Day, a Garage

A proper shop would make life easier, especially through the winter. I'd love that someday. For now, this is a driveway-based business, not a travelling mobile service. Please contact me first to arrange a visit; my home address isn't posted publicly.

Christ Centered

My Christian faith matters to how I want to run this business. I'm not a perfect man, but I want to be honest about repairs, treat people fairly and work to the glory of God.
