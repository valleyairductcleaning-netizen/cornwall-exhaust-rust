Undercoating for Cornwall Winters

Road salt, wet weather and winter driving can accelerate corrosion on metal underneath a vehicle. Undercoating is one way to help protect exposed areas and slow future rust.

What We Look At Before Applying Undercoating

We consider the condition of the underside, existing rust, areas that need attention and whether the surface is suitable for the product being used. Undercoating does not restore metal that has already rusted away.

Why Preparation Matters

Loose scale, trapped moisture and severely damaged areas can affect how well a protective coating works. We discuss the condition of your vehicle and what preparation is appropriate before quoting the work.

Undercoating vs. Rust Repair

Undercoating is intended to protect; rust repair deals with existing damage. If you have holes, weak metal or damaged parts, those concerns should be assessed separately.
