Muffler Repair in Cornwall, Ontario

A loud muffler does not always mean the entire exhaust system needs replacing. We look at the condition of the muffler and its connections first.

Request a Quote

Why Is My Muffler So Loud?

A rust hole, split seam, damaged joint or broken pipe near the muffler can change how your vehicle sounds. The right fix depends on where the leak is and how much solid metal remains.

Muffler Repair or Replacement?

If the muffler body is badly corroded, replacement is often the sensible choice. If the problem is a repairable connection or adjacent pipe, a more limited repair may be possible. Tim explains the options rather than automatically recommending an entire system.

Signs You May Need Muffler Work

  • Your vehicle suddenly sounds louder than normal.
  • You hear a rattle from underneath.
  • A muffler or tailpipe is loose or hanging.
  • Rust has damaged a joint or connection.
